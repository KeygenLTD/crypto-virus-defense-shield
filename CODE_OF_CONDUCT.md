# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background.

## Our Standards
Be respectful, constructive, and defensive-minded. No malware distribution, no harassment, no spam.

## Enforcement
Instances may be reported to the maintainers at `KeygenLTD` via GitHub Issues. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
